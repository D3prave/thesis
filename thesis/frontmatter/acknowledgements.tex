I hereby declare that I have written this thesis independently and have
not used any sources or aids other than those cited. All passages taken
verbatim or in substance from other works are marked as such, and the
sources are listed in the bibliography. I further declare that

results, or substantive content without subsequent verification. The
thesis has not previously been submitted, in this or in a similar form,
to any other examination authority.
