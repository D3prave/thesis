\chapter{Additional Robustness Checks}
\label{app:robustness}

These supplementary checks reuse the recorded answers and correctness labels.
The analysis choices were recorded before inspecting the sensitivities, and
results were preserved in commits \texttt{643f047} and \texttt{edc22f0}. No answers were regenerated
and no probes were retrained. Full outputs, input hashes, and reproduction
instructions are in \path{results/robustness_20260911/} and
\path{docs/robustness_results_2026-09-12.md}. All 3,564 corresponding saved AUROCs
were reproduced to their six-decimal export precision.

\section{Shared questions and statistical units}

Each of the 24 semantic-versus-surface comparisons was evaluated with 2,000
bootstrap draws, seed 20260911. Independent record and hierarchical resampling
serve as references. The additional fixed-grid scheme shares each dataset's
question draw across generators and seeds. The crossed scheme also resamples
the three datasets and three generators independently, weighting each pair
by the product of its two multiplicities. Seeds are averaged within pairs.
Only three levels of each factor are observed; the crossed ranges are a
sensitivity check, not precise guarantees of population-level coverage.

Table~\ref{tab:robustness-bootstrap} reports these bootstrap comparisons.

With crossed resampling, Qwen entailment retains a positive sentence-answer
range under both judges: $[+0.0452,+0.1394]$ for Qwen grading and
$[+0.0391,+0.1498]$ for Llama grading. Its short-phrase ranges include zero,
as do both DeBERTa sentence comparisons. All six separate dataset or generator
omissions retain the Qwen sentence advantage under both judges; smaller
DeBERTa advantages can change sign. The Qwen short-phrase point difference
also stays positive under each omission, which is a different perturbation
from joint factor and question resampling.

No LLM-graded cell AUROC and no aggregate draw was undefined. Across schemes
and comparisons, token-F1's maximum invalid-cell fraction was 0.4741\,\%;
at most 11.85\,\% of draws contained an invalid cell. These maxima occur in
different comparisons. Undefined cells are omitted before seed averaging;
a pair with no valid seeds is omitted from that draw. All omission rates
are exported, and no chance AUROC is substituted.

\section{Length, termination, and probe targets}

Longer character count was fixed as the uncertainty direction for two
length-only baselines. With the default entailment model and Qwen grading,
mean graded-answer-length AUROC is 0.5803 for sentences and 0.5432 for short
phrases; mean sampled-answer-length AUROC is 0.5975 and 0.5976. Both are below
DSE's corresponding 0.7568 and 0.8059. Splitting each cell at its median graded
answer length gives shorter/longer DSE-minus-surface differences of
$+0.0100/+0.0313$ for sentences and $+0.0024/-0.0076$ for short phrases.
All 27 cells have both classes in these length strata. These associations do
not isolate a causal effect of length.

The recorded sample-cap field counts sequences without EOS/EOT termination.
Stored text can subsequently be shortened at a stop string, so the field does
not measure the fraction of visibly incomplete answers. Flagged-sample rates
average 47.4--83.0\,\% across the four prompting conditions. The strata with
no flagged samples are often sparse or single-class: only 18 of 27 sentence
cells and 6 of 27 original short-phrase cells have defined AUROC under Qwen
grading. Their changing eligible sets prevent a clean estimate of truncation's
effect. Counts and all grader-specific diagnostics remain in the exports.

The 54 saved SEP evaluation cells were also checked against their intended
high/low entropy target. Each target uses DSE greater than or equal to its
saved training threshold. Fit hashes, training-only threshold provenance,
dataset, generator, seed, and evaluation entropy-source hashes all match.
No threshold was selected on evaluation questions. Mean target AUROC is
0.7103 for sentences and 0.8389 for short phrases; balanced accuracy at
probability 0.5 is 0.6457 and 0.7324. Corresponding Qwen-graded incorrectness
AUROCs are 0.6723 and 0.7651. Predicting entropy is therefore distinct from
predicting correctness. This check does not validate factual labels or resolve
the accuracy probe's historical training-label provenance.

\section{Normalization and answer order}

A bounded check selected 30 questions per dataset by a fixed hash rule,
shared across three generators and the two original conditions, at seed 42:
540 records in total. DeBERTa-v3-large at the recorded revision labeled raw
and normalized answer pairs. All 540 raw-answer partitions were reproduced
exactly. Normalization changed the original-order partition for 138 of 270
sentence records and 33 of 270 short-phrase records. Thus small aggregate
AUROC differences can conceal substantial changes to individual groups.

Fifty per-record order permutations reused these fixed entailment judgments.
Raw-answer entropy changed under at least one tested order for 96 of 270
sentence records and 22 of 270 short-phrase records. Mean per-record entropy
ranges were 0.2081 and 0.0401 nats. Across the 50 replicates, mean Qwen-graded
raw DSE AUROC ranged from 0.7761 to 0.8177 for sentences and 0.7528 to 0.7803
for short phrases. These are observed permutation ranges, not confidence
intervals or exhaustive bounds over all possible orders.

\section{Number of sampled answers}

For $M=2,4,6,8$, 50 subsets per record were drawn without replacement from
the original ten answers, preserving their relative order. Raw DSE, normalized
DSE, and surface entropy use identical subsets. Clustering was recomputed
within each subset; simply selecting the original cluster IDs would not
reproduce the algorithm. Entropy contributions are summed in sorted count
order to preserve ties across cluster-ID permutations. At $M=10$, the original answers were used once.

Table~\ref{tab:robustness-samples} reports the resulting AUROCs.
\input{includes/t_robustness_samples.tex}

On this subset, using ten answers improves discrimination over using two
for all three estimators. Surface entropy benefits as well, so the improvement
cannot be attributed solely to semantic grouping. The check neither establishes
that ten is optimal nor measures performance above ten. The fixed subset,
single seed, and single entailment model also limit extrapolation to the
complete experiment. Both LLM graders retain all nine cells; token-F1 omits
one single-class sentence cell. All three graders, per-cell eligibility,
replicates, and ranges are retained in the result package.

The bounded entailment job evaluated 31,498 unique directed pairs after
exact-duplicate elimination and completed in 5 minutes 19 seconds on one
A100 GPU. Cached labels make the normalization, order, and sample-count
analyses reproducible without further inference. This runtime covers one
validation job and is not an end-to-end cost comparison of the methods.
